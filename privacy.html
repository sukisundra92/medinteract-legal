How your data is processed Drug interaction data is retrieved from the U.S. National Library of Medicine's DailyMed database (public, government-run). Your medication names are sent to this API to retrieve interaction information. AI explanations are generated using Google's Gemini API. The medication names and interaction text are sent to Google's servers to produce the plain-language output. Google's privacy policy applies to this processing: policies.google.com/privacy Subscription management is handled by RevenueCat. RevenueCat receives your anonymous user ID and subscription status. They do not receive your medication data. RevenueCat's privacy policy: revenuecat.com/privacy 3. Where your data is stored Supabase (hosted by Supabase Inc., US-based infrastructure). Your data is encrypted at rest and in transit (TLS 1.3). We do not sell, rent, or share your medication data with third parties for advertising or any commercial purpose. 4. Deletion is permanent and completed within 30 days. Object to processing or restrict processing by contacting us at [your email] If you are in Canada, you have additional rights under PIPEDA. If you are in the EU/UK, you have rights under GDPR. Contact us to exercise any of these rights. 5. Children's privacy The App is not intended for children under 13.
