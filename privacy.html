2. How your data is processed

  • Drug interaction data is retrieved from the U.S. National Library of Medicine's DailyMed database (public, government-run). Your medication names are sent to this API to retrieve interaction information.
  • AI explanations are generated using Google's Gemini API. The medication names and interaction text are sent to Google's servers to produce the plain-language output. Google's privacy policy applies to this processing: policies.google.com/privacy
  • Subscription management is handled by RevenueCat. RevenueCat receives your anonymous user ID and subscription status. They do not receive your medication data. RevenueCat's privacy policy: revenuecat.com/privacy

3. Where your data is stored

  • Supabase (hosted by Supabase Inc., US-based infrastructure). Your data is encrypted at rest and in transit (TLS 1.3).
  • We do not sell, rent, or share your medication data with third parties for advertising or any commercial purpose.

4. Your rights

You can, at any time:

  • View your data in-app (Settings → My Data)
  • Export your data as a CSV file
  • Delete your account and all associated data (Settings → Delete Account). Deletion is permanent and completed within 30 days.
  • Object to processing or restrict processing by contacting us at [your email]

If you are in Canada, you have additional rights under PIPEDA. If you are in the EU/UK, you have rights under GDPR. Contact us to exercise any of these rights.

5. Children's privacy

The App is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, contact us and we will delete it.

6. Data retention

  • Active accounts: data is retained as long as your account is active.
  • After deletion: data is permanently erased from Supabase within 30 days.
  • Crash logs: retained for 90 days, then deleted.

7. Data security

  • All data is encrypted in transit (TLS) and at rest (AES-256)
  • Access to your data is restricted to you and our technical infrastructure
  • We do not use your data to train AI models
