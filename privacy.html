Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 11, 2026

1. Services we use

  • Newly Backend operates the app's automatic anonymous accounts and authenticated online features. You do not need to supply an email address or password to use the current account flow.
  • Newly AI and the AI service providers processing those requests handle optional Nurse Bear conversations and medication-label scanning.
  • NIH/NLM RxNav is used for medication-name searches, and RxCheck supplements medication interaction information. Some interaction checks use a database stored within the app and run on your device.
  • RevenueCat manages subscription validation, purchase restoration, and access to subscription features. Apple or Google processes subscription payments through your store account. MedInteract+ does not receive your payment-card number.

2. Information sharing

Your profiles, medication and food lists, schedules, appointments, chat history, and privacy settings are stored on your device. Specific information also leaves your device when you use the following features:

  • Nurse Bear: If you allow AI chat sharing, we send your current message, up to ten recent messages, the active profile's first name and child-mode setting, medication names, types, doses, dosing frequency, food instructions, foods, and interaction findings for AI processing.
  • Label scanning: If you separately allow AI photo sharing, we send your selected or captured label photo for AI processing. The photo may contain names, prescription information, pharmacy details, or other personal and health information.
  • Response reporting: If you confirm a report, we send the selected assistant response and your selected reason to the developer for safety review. The report is stored with a response identifier, submission time, and review status, and is associated with your anonymous account. Other conversation messages, your medication list, and photos are not automatically attached. The selected response may itself contain personal or health information.
  • Medication searches and interaction requests: RxNav receives the medication search text you enter. RxCheck receives the two medication names being checked; the app does not include your profile name, doses, chat history, or photos in that request.
  • Authentication and subscriptions: These services process account or customer identifiers, session information, and purchase records needed to operate the app. Authentication records include technical client information and may include an IP address when available.
AI chat and photo sharing are separate permissions and are off by default. You can decline either permission and continue using manual medication entry and on-device interaction checks.
We do NOT collect:
  • Payment card details (handled entirely by Apple/Google)
  • Location data
  • Contacts or calendar access

3. Retention

  • Information stored on your device remains in the app's storage until it is removed through the app's controls or its storage is otherwise cleared.
  • Submitted response reports have no automatic expiration. They remain in our application records until removed by us or through successful deletion of the associated app account.
  • Authentication services retain account and session records. Subscription services retain purchase and entitlement records under their applicable policies.
  • AI processing providers and other online services may retain submitted information and operational records under their applicable policies. Exact retention periods and deletion arrangements for all such records have not yet been confirmed. We therefore do not promise that AI inputs or photos are never stored, are deleted immediately, or are excluded from model training.

4. Your controls and deletion

You can allow or withdraw AI chat and photo sharing separately through Profile → AI privacy. Withdrawal stops future requests and cancels a pending request where possible. It cannot recall information already transmitted or guarantee that processing already started by a service provider is stopped.

To delete your app data, use Profile → Delete all my data and confirm the request. The app first requests deletion of your anonymous account and its submitted response reports. After the service confirms success, the app clears its locally stored profiles, medication and food lists, appointments, chat history, scan draft, and AI permissions. If server deletion fails, local data is preserved so you can retry.

Limitations: This action does not delete original photos from your photo library or copies of reports you exported elsewhere. It does not establish deletion of every third-party processing record, purchase record, or backup. Deleting app data does not cancel a subscription. Manage or cancel subscriptions through your Apple or Google subscription settings.

5. Data security

  • All data is encrypted in transit (TLS) and at rest (AES-256)
  • Access to your data is restricted to you and our technical infrastructure

6. Children's privacy

The App is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, contact us and we will delete it.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy at any time. Material changes will be notified in-app or via email.
